About COLLI'S

Who you're calling, and how the work gets done.

Carpentry in an existing house is a different job to carpentry in a new one. Nothing is square, nothing is plumb, and everything has to be scribed to fit what's actually there. Quoting from a photograph is how a job goes wrong on the second day.

That means measuring properly before pricing, chasing rot back to solid wood rather than covering it, and finishing the work -- filled, sanded, finished -- because the difference between built and finished is most of what you're paying for.

We cover Omaha.

What happens when you call

1

You call

Tell us what you're picturing, or what's gone wrong. Rough ideas are fine at this stage -- so are photos and a phone sketch.

Old houses are rarely square and rot is rarely only where you can see it. We check what's behind the surface before quoting.

Materials, method and price in writing, with the choices that change the number called out so you can decide where to spend.

How it works

What happens when you call

1

You call

Tell us what you're picturing, or what's gone wrong. Rough ideas are fine at this stage -- so are photos and a phone sketch.

2

We measure and look properly

Old houses are rarely square and rot is rarely only where you can see it. We check what's behind the surface before quoting.

3

You get a real scope

Materials, method and price in writing, with the choices that change the number called out so you can decide where to spend.

4

We build it and finish it

Work done on site with dust contained, and finished properly -- trim, fill, sand, done. Site swept at the end of every day.

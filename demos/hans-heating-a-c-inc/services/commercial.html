Commercial

Rooftop units and light commercial systems.

Rooftop units and light commercial systems live a harder life than residential equipment and are usually keeping a business trading. Economisers, controls, belts and condensate handling are the things that fail, and a failure normally costs the business more than the repair does.

Signs you need this

  • Uneven temperatures across a space
  • A rooftop unit short-cycling or tripping
  • Rising energy cost without a usage change
  • Equipment past service life with no plan for replacing it

What to expect

Scheduled service around trading hours, findings reported plainly, and repair-or-replace advice that accounts for what downtime costs you.

How it works

What happens when you call

1

You call

Tell us what the system is doing -- not cooling, short-cycling, blowing warm, making a noise it didn't used to make. Age and model help.

2

We diagnose properly

Readings, not guesses: pressures, temperature split, airflow and electrical. A system low on refrigerant has a leak, and topping it up without finding the leak is a repair that fails again.

3

You get the options

Usually there is a repair option and a replace option, and the honest answer depends on the age of the equipment and what it costs to keep it running. You get both, with the numbers.

4

We install or repair it right

Sized to the house, not to the box that came out. Ductwork, airflow and startup readings checked before we call it done.

About JAY MOORE LANDSCAPING

Who you're calling, and how the work gets done.

Most landscaping disappointment comes from plants that were never going to survive where they were put, and from schedules that slip until the property is playing catch-up. Neither is really a gardening problem.

Working the other way round -- looking at the sun, soil, grade and drainage first, then choosing what suits it, then keeping to a schedule you can count on -- is unglamorous and it's the whole difference between a property that improves each year and one that needs redoing.

We cover Omaha.

How it works

What happens when you call

1

You call

Tell us what you want -- regular upkeep, a one-off cleanup, or a yard you'd like redesigned. Photos help if you have them.

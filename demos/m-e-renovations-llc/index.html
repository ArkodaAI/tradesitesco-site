Common questions

Straight answers

A door has started sticking. Is that serious?

Often it's seasonal movement, and sometimes it's the frame going out of square as the house settles. Which one it is shows up in whether the gap is even along the top and side -- worth looking at, because planing a door that's telling you about a structural movement just hides it.

Is my deck safe?

The parts that fail are usually the ones nobody looks at: the ledger where it attaches to the house, its flashing, and the post bases at ground level. Surface boards look bad long before they're dangerous; the connections are what matter.

Can rot be patched, or does it all have to come out?

It has to be cut back to solid wood. Filling or covering rot leaves the moisture source and the decay behind the repair, and it comes back bigger. Finding out why it stayed wet matters as much as the wood.

How much of a remodel is disruptive?

That depends on the room and how far back it goes. Dust containment, water and power interruptions and where you'll cook or wash are worth planning before the first day, not on it.

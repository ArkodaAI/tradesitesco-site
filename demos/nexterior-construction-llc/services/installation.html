Installation

New work fitted properly the first time, cleaned up after.

New work is worth doing once and properly. Preparation, fixings, and all the parts that get covered up and never looked at again are what decide whether the thing is still solid in five years, and none of them show up in a photograph of the finished job. That is exactly why they are the first things to get skipped when a price gets cut, and why the cheapest installation is so often the one that gets redone soonest.

Signs you need this

  • New equipment or fittings to install
  • A replacement for something at the end of its life
  • Work another trade has left part-finished
  • A project you want done properly rather than quickly

What to expect

The job assessed, the scope written down, and the work done and checked before it's called finished.

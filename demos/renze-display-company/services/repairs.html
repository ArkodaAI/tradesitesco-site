Repairs

Fast, tidy repair work with the price agreed before we start.

Repair work goes wrong in one predictable way: the symptom gets treated and the cause is left sitting there. Finding out why something failed is usually the whole difference between a repair that holds and one you pay for twice, and it is the part that gets skipped when a job is priced to be the cheapest quote rather than the right one. It is nearly always quicker to find the cause than to return to the same problem.

Signs you need this

  • Something that has failed once and then failed again
  • A problem that keeps coming back in the same place
  • Damage after a leak, a storm or an impact
  • A repair someone else did that hasn't held

What to expect

A proper look before a price, the cause explained, and the price agreed before work starts.

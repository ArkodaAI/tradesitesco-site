Common questions

Straight answers

Can you tell if my roof is damaged just by looking from the ground?

Usually not. Hail bruising, lifted nails, cracked boot seals and failed flashing are invisible from the driveway and are exactly what causes leaks. A proper look means getting on the roof.

There's a stain on my ceiling. Is that where the leak is?

Almost never. Water runs along the underside of the decking and down rafters before it drops, so the wet spot inside can be several feet from the actual entry point. That's why leak tracing matters more than patching.

Should I repair or replace?

It depends on the age of the roof and how much of it is failing. One flashing detail on a ten-year-old roof is a repair. Granule loss across the whole slope on a twenty-year-old roof is money spent twice if you patch it.

How long does a replacement take?

Most houses are a one- to three-day job once materials are on site, weather permitting. Bigger or steeper roofs and full decking replacement take longer.

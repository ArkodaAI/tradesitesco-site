Gutters

Gutter repair and replacement so water leaves the roof properly.

Gutters are part of the roof system, and when they fail the damage shows up somewhere else -- rotted fascia, soaked soffits, a wet basement, or settlement where water has been dumping against the foundation for years. Undersized, badly pitched or disconnected gutters cause more expensive problems than they look capable of.

Signs you need this

  • Water sheeting over the front edge during rain
  • Staining or peeling paint on the fascia behind the gutter
  • Downspouts discharging right at the foundation
  • Sagging runs or gutters pulling away from the house

What to expect

We look at the whole path the water takes -- sizing, pitch, hangers and where the downspouts actually let go -- rather than only the piece that's visibly broken.

How it works

What happens when you call

1

You call

Tell us what you're seeing -- a stain on the ceiling, missing shingles after a storm, or a roof that's simply old. We'll tell you on the phone whether it sounds urgent.

2

Most roof problems cannot be diagnosed from the ground. We walk the roof, check the flashing, valleys, boots and decking, and photograph what we find.

A written scope: what's wrong, what we'd do about it, and what it comes to. You get the photos too, whether or not you hire us.

4

Materials delivered, tear-off contained, and a magnet run over the yard and drive before we leave. You should not be finding nails in your lawn in spring.

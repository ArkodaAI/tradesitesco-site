Decks &amp; Porches

Outdoor structures built to code and built to last.

Decks fail at their connections, not their surfaces. The ledger where it fastens to the house and the flashing above it are the details that matter, along with post bases at ground level -- and all three are hidden. Grey, splitting boards look far worse than they are; a poorly attached ledger looks fine right up until it doesn't.

Signs you need this

  • Movement or bounce when you walk across it
  • Soft wood at the posts or where it meets the house
  • Rusted or missing fasteners at the ledger
  • Railings that flex when leaned on

What to expect

The structure and its connections checked before the boards, a clear answer on repair versus rebuild, and the reasoning shown rather than asserted.

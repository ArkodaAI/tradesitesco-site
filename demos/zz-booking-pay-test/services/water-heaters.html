Water Heaters

Repair, replacement and tankless conversions.

Tank heaters fail in a fairly predictable way: sediment builds on the bottom, the anode rod is consumed, and eventually the tank itself corrodes through. The failure that costs real money is the one that happens while nobody is home, which is why the warning signs are worth acting on.

Signs you need this

  • Rumbling or popping while it heats -- sediment on the tank floor
  • Rusty or discoloured hot water
  • Moisture, rust or a puddle around the base
  • Running out of hot water sooner than it used to

What's included

  • Tank & tankless
  • Gas and electric
  • Warranties honored

What to expect

An honest read on whether it's worth servicing or replacing, the tank-versus-tankless trade-off explained in terms of your house, and sizing based on how much hot water you actually use.

Common questions

Straight answers

Water is coming out right now. What do I do first?

Shut it off. Every house has a main shutoff -- typically where the supply enters, near the meter or the water heater -- and individual fixtures usually have their own valve underneath. Turn it off, then call.

Why does my drain keep clogging in the same place?

A drain that re-clogs in the same spot usually has a reason: roots into a joint, a belly holding water, or a fitting catching debris. A camera down the line shows which, and that decides whether it needs clearing or repairing.

How long should a water heater last?

Tank heaters commonly run eight to twelve years, and tankless longer. Rumbling, rusty water, or moisture at the base means it's near the end -- a tank that fails while you're out does far more damage than a planned replacement.

My water pressure has dropped everywhere. What causes that?

House-wide pressure loss usually points to the supply side -- a failing pressure regulator, a partly closed valve, or corrosion in older galvanised piping. One slow fixture is a different problem: usually an aerator or a cartridge.

How it works

What happens when you call

1

You call

Describe the symptom -- a breaker that keeps tripping, lights that dim when the AC starts, outlets that stopped working, or a project you're planning.

2

We trace it

Electrical faults hide. We test the circuit rather than replacing parts until the symptom goes away, because a breaker that trips is usually doing its job.

3

You get a clear scope

What needs doing, what's code-required alongside it, and what it costs -- in writing, before anything is opened up.

4

We do it safely and tidily

Work done to code, circuits labelled properly, and permits pulled where the work calls for them.

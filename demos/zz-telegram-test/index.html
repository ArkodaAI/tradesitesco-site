Common questions

Straight answers

A breaker keeps tripping. Can it just be replaced with a bigger one?

No, and it matters: the breaker is sized to protect the wire behind it. Fitting a larger one lets the wire heat past what it's rated for, which is a fire risk. A breaker that trips repeatedly is reporting a real problem -- an overloaded circuit, a failing appliance, or a fault.

My lights dim when the air conditioner starts.

A brief dip is normal on a large motor start. A noticeable, repeated dim can mean an overloaded service, a loose connection, or an undersized feed -- worth testing rather than living with, because loose connections generate heat.

Do I need a panel upgrade?

Usually driven by capacity or by the panel itself. Adding heavy loads -- an EV charger, a hot tub, a shop, central air -- can exceed what an older service can carry. Some older panel brands are also known to fail and are worth replacing on their own account.

What does an EV charger need?

A Level 2 charger needs its own dedicated circuit sized to the charger, and the house's existing load has to be calculated to check the service can carry it. Where the car parks relative to the panel drives most of the cost.

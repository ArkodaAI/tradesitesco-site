EV Chargers

Home charger circuits installed to code.

A Level 2 charger is a dedicated circuit sized to the charger, plus a load calculation to confirm the existing service can carry it. Most of what varies in the cost is the run: where the car parks relative to the panel, and what the cable has to pass through to get there.

Signs you need this

  • A new EV, or one on order
  • Charging from a standard outlet and finding it too slow
  • A detached garage or a long run from the panel
  • An older service you're not sure has the capacity

What to expect

Load calculated first, the route and any service work priced clearly, and the install done to code with the right protection.

Electrical faults hide. We test the circuit rather than replacing parts until the symptom goes away, because a breaker that trips is usually doing its job.

Questions

The things everybody asks

How is this only $499?
Because we don't have an office, a sales team, or account managers. Our process is tight and we build a lot of sites for the same kinds of businesses, so we're not reinventing the wheel every time. You're paying for the website, not for somebody's overhead.
Do I really not pay anything up front?
Correct. We build your complete site and send you a private link. You look at it, you ask for changes, and only when you're happy do you pay the $499. If you don't like it, you don't pay and we both move on with no hard feelings.
Is there a monthly fee?
Not a required one. Your first year of hosting is included in the $499. After that it's $29/month if you want us to keep hosting and maintaining it — or you can take the files and host it wherever you like for a few dollars a month. Your call, and we'll tell you honestly which makes more sense for you.
What if I already have a website?
Then we'll tell you honestly whether replacing it is worth $499. Sometimes the answer is no — sometimes yours just needs a click-to-call button and better wording, and we'll say so. We'd rather tell you the truth than take money for something you don't need.
Do I need my own domain name?
If you have one, we'll use it. If you don't, we'll help you register one in your name for about $12/year, paid directly to the registrar. It stays yours no matter what happens with us.
What if I don't have any photos?
Very common, and not a problem. We use professional stock imagery for the trade until you get us real ones — and honestly, a few phone photos of your truck, your crew and a finished job beat stock every time. Send them whenever; we'll swap them in free.
Will I be able to change things myself?
Yes. We hand you a simple editor for text, photos, hours and prices, plus a short video showing you how. Or email us and we'll do it — free on the Care Plan, and $49 for anything larger without it.
Will this actually get me on Google?
Here's the honest version. We do everything right at setup: your pages are structured properly, your business info is marked up so Google understands it, your service areas each get a page, and your site is submitted to Google. That's the foundation, and most trade sites don't have it. But nobody can promise a #1 ranking, and anyone who does is selling you something. The single biggest local ranking factor is your Google Business Profile — which is why we offer to set that up too, for $79.
Will AI assistants like ChatGPT recommend my business?
Nobody can promise that, and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. Here's what we actually do: we describe your business in schema.org structured data so machines read facts instead of guessing, we answer real customer questions in plain sentences an assistant can quote, we add an llms.txt summary file, and we set your robots.txt to explicitly welcome AI crawlers — which most sites block by accident. That's the difference between being readable and being invisible. A business an AI can't read is never the answer it gives. It's included at no extra cost, because we think in two years it stops being optional.
How long does it really take?
Five business days from the day you send your answers. Rush delivery is 48 hours for $149. The clock starts when we get your six answers, not when you first email us — so the fastest thing you can do is answer them.
What do you need from me?
Six answers, once. Your business name and phone number, what services you offer, the towns you serve, your hours, whether you're licensed and insured, and anything you want customers to know. That's it. No calls, no meetings, no weekly check-ins.
